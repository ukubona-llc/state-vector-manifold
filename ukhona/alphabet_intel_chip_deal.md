# Case-Study: Alphabet’s Intel Chip Deal Reframes AI Capacity, Valuation And Cash Flow Risks

Alphabet has reportedly ordered more than 3 million custom AI chips from Intel for delivery in 2028. 

The contract marks Alphabet’s first large scale AI chip agreement with Intel, expanding beyond its in house designs and TSMC manufacturing. 

This follows Alphabet raising about $85b to fund AI infrastructure as demand for AI capacity continues to strain global chip supply. 

Alphabet, the parent of NasdaqGS:GOOGL, is committing significant capital to AI hardware, with the stock trading at $363.31 and up 15.3% year to date. The share price has also gained 106.9% over the past year and 199.3% over five years, which highlights how closely investors are watching its AI strategy. In that context, a multi year chip order with Intel indicates the importance of hardware security and capacity for Alphabet’s core AI platforms. 

For investors, the Intel deal adds a new piece to the puzzle around AI execution risk, supply chain resilience and future capital needs. The agreement also connects to broader questions about how much of Alphabet’s recent $85b capital raise will flow into external chip partners versus its own custom silicon, and how that mix could influence margins and competitive positioning in AI services.
